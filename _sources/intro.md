# Welcome to the ACIAR Species Distribution Modelling portal.

This portal contains information on the historic and future climate of Southeast Asia, and a case study of the impacts of projected climate change on the forestry pest species *Leptocybe invasa*.

You can navigate using the menu on the left hand side, or the buttons below the page contents.

This site was produced as part of ACIAR FST/2018/179, lead by the Climate Futures Research Group at the University of Tasmania, partnering with the Center for Climate Risk and Opportunity Management (CCROM), IPB University, Indonesia.
The site contains work developed by Kathleen Beyer, Ben Weeding, Gito Sugih Immanuel, Sisi Febriyanti Muin, Syamsu Dwi Jadmiko, Yanmarshus Bachtiar, I Putu Santikayasa, and Rizaldi Boer.

For help with the portal and the data, please contact Dr Ben Weeding at ben.weeding@utas.edu.au
